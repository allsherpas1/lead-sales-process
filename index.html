How Leads Reach You

Leads are matched to accounts in HubSpot based on their email domain, then synced to Salesforce and assigned to the owner of the matching Master Account. When a new lead is assigned to you, you will receive:

✅A Task in Salesforce
📧An Email Notification
🔔An In-App Salesforce Notification

How to Close Out a Task

Once you've taken action on a lead, you must update and close the task in Salesforce within 1 business day. Here's how:

  1. 1
    Open the task from your dashboard, email notification, or in-app alert.
  2. 2
    Click Edit on the task record.
  3. 3
    Update the Comments field with a description of the action you took — e.g. "Left voicemail, will follow up Friday" or "Sent intro email."
  4. 4
    Choose a Disposition that reflects the outcome of your outreach.
  5. 5
    Change the Status to Completed and save.
