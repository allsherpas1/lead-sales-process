Sales Training Reference — May 2026

How Leads Reach You

Leads are matched to accounts in HubSpot based on their email domain, then synced to Salesforce and assigned to the owner of the matching Master Account. When a new lead is assigned to you, you will receive:

✅A Task in Salesforce
📧An Email Notification
🔔An In-App Salesforce Notification

The Three Lead Paths

1
Marketing Qualified Lead (MQL)
Trigger: Contact accumulates 100+ engagement points from website activity
How it works A contact browsing the Genesee Scientific website accumulates points by visiting key pages. When they reach a score of 100, they are automatically designated as an MQL, synced to Salesforce, and a task is created for the Master Account owner.
Contact hits 100 pts
→
Syncs to Salesforce
→
Task created → Account Owner
→
Email + In-App notification

2
Meeting Scheduled
Trigger: Contact books a meeting via your HubSpot scheduling link
How it works As part of the ABM campaign, contacts associated with targeted accounts are emailed with a CTA to schedule a meeting directly with their sales rep. When they book, the contact syncs to Salesforce, a calendar event is created, and a task is assigned to the rep.
Contact books via scheduling link
→
Syncs to Salesforce
→
Calendar event created
→
Task → Account Owner

3
ABM LinkedIn Form Submission
Trigger: Contact submits a form after clicking a LinkedIn ad
How it works Contacts associated with ABM-targeted accounts are served LinkedIn ads. The ads link to a landing page with a form requesting more information. When the form is submitted, the contact syncs to Salesforce and a task is created for the account owner.
